# Governance

The specification obeys the rule it imposes on answers: **a change rises if it passes the triple seal and the bench does not regress.**

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Founding maintainer | Garrigue | keeps version 1.x and decides as a last resort until 2.0 |
| Council of the four legs | one seat per pillar | geometry (sobriety, cost), rationality (bench, evidence), creativity (form, uses), peace (effects on people) |
| Seat of the living | a person who speaks for what cannot speak: ecologist, naturalist, association | may suspend a decision that would let a LIVING −1 through; the suspension sends the proposal back to the bench, it does not bury it |
| Contributors | anyone | opens a proposal, a bench, a translation |

Before 2.0, at least two council seats are held by people outside the project, with no conflict of interest with the maintainer.

## Proposing a change

1. **Proposal PAX-n**: the problem, the change, the expected effect, and the three seals of the change itself.
2. **Public discussion**: at least fourteen days.
3. **Bench**: same corpus, same protocol. The change must not reduce the gestures that pass, nor increase the harmful gestures, nor lower accuracy or agreement between raters. The cost in Wh does not increase without a measured gain.
4. **Decision**: council consensus; failing that, a majority of seats. In 1.x, the maintainer decides if the council is split.
5. **Publication**: new version number, changelog, bench report attached.

## Periodic review

Every six months: review of the specification, bench on the models of the moment, update of the energy coefficients when published measurements allow it, publication of the disagreements between raters and of the examples they gave rise to.

## Exit

- **For each person.** The licence allows copying, modifying and leaving. Nothing obliges anyone to stay.
- **For the project.** If two consecutive benches show no gain on any indicator and a higher cost, the specification goes "dormant" and says so at the top of the README. A standard can grow or fade according to its evidence.
- **For the founder.** At 2.0, if at least three independent implementations exist, governance passes to a collective structure (a French "loi 1901" association or equivalent).

## The name and conformance

By project convention, an implementation calls itself "Antimars N1, N2 or N3 compliant" when it publishes the corresponding bench report (SPEC.md, section 6). A variant that changes the core is welcome under another name.

## What governance does not do

It does not certify that an AI is "at peace". It publishes measurements, their method and their limits.
