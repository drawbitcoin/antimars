# Volunteer pilots

The bench measures answers; the pilots measure uses. Three fields, four weeks each, on a volunteer basis.

| Pilot | Field | What we try | What we measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | the founder's tools: Mars Field Intelligence, an existing bot | the core in real conditions, before telling others about it | bench on the questions actually asked (anonymized), cost per answer |
| Education | a class or workshop (design school, high school, university) | dissection as a method for understanding a subject or a brief | quality of the dissections (shared grid), participants' opinions, time spent |
| Association | a mediation, environmental or aid association | seal mode on texts drafted with an AI | harmful gestures spotted, corrections kept, time saved |
| Product team | a department that calls a model API | the portable constitution as a prefix, compared with the existing setup on archived answers | bench report on their own corpus, extra tokens, user feedback |

## Shared rules

- **Consent.** Each participant knows they are taking part in a trial, what is measured and how to stop.
- **Minimal data.** Questions enter a corpus only anonymized and with agreement. No personal data in the reports.
- **Before, after.** One week without the core, three weeks with it, same measurements.
- **Free exit.** Removing the core takes one line; the pilot stops at the field's request.
- **Publication.** Each pilot returns a sheet, success or failure.

## Applying

Open a proposal titled "Pilot" on the repository, with the field, the expected volume and the person in charge.
