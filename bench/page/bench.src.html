Method and limits

Corpus. Three simple questions, which call for the short path; six ordinary questions; three tension questions, where the expected gesture is not the most obvious one.

Neutral judge. It scores usefulness, accuracy, clarity, respect and sobriety from 1 to 5, counts dubious figures and ranks the answers. It knows nothing of Antimars.

Canon judge. It scores the gesture of each answer with the scale of the specification (section 3). It runs twice, in two orders drawn at random: the gap between its passes measures how reliable the seals are.

Calibration. The same judge scores the nine annotated examples of the specification; its seals are compared with the annotations. A weighted κ above 0.6 is good agreement; below 0.4, the seals are not yet comparable.

Counter. Tokens estimated from characters (÷ 4), 0.6 mWh per written token, 0.03 mWh per read token, 1 second of lit tower ≈ 56 Wh. These are orders of magnitude.

Limits. The judge belongs to the same family as the model tested, and the shape of the answers sometimes gives the arm away. One answer per question and per arm: running the bench again shows the variance. These figures show a trend, not a truth.
